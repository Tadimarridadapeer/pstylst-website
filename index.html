About P’STYLST

P’STYLST is Personal Stylist Intelligence designed to make personal styling more personalized, visual, and practical. It helps users understand their appearance, discover styling guidance, explore outfits and hairstyles, organize their wardrobe, save looks, and plan what they want to wear.

Style should feel personal.

Personal style is more than following trends. It is about understanding what works for you — your proportions, features, colors, preferences, wardrobe, and the way you want to express yourself.

P’STYLST brings these elements together into one personalized styling experience, helping users move from understanding themselves to discovering and planning styles that feel right for them.

Our Vision

To make personal styling intelligent, visual, and accessible — helping everyone understand what suits them and express their individuality with confidence.

We envision a future where personal styling is not limited to trends, guesswork, or generic recommendations. Instead, technology can help people understand themselves better and make more confident choices about how they look and feel.

Our Mission

To combine personal insights, styling intelligence, visual exploration, and wardrobe organization into one personalized experience that helps people discover, create, and plan styles that truly work for them.

P’STYLST brings together appearance analysis, personalized styling guidance, outfit exploration, hairstyle inspiration, digital wardrobe organization, and look planning to make the styling journey simpler, more visual, and more personal.
